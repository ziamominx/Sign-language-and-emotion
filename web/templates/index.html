About live recognition

The pretrained model recognizes isolated signs from a 2,000 word ASL research vocabulary. Confident guesses enter your message automatically; uncertain ones stay as suggestions. It cannot translate continuous ASL grammar or every sign, and guesses can be wrong. Review the message before using it for important communication.
