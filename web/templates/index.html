About live recognition

Hand landmarks show tracking feedback. Hold both open hands for 1.5 seconds to speak the collected words as a sentence; the gesture does not add a new word. The facial expression label is an uncertain visual estimate, not a measure of what someone feels. ASL words uses an isolated 2,000-word research vocabulary; fingerspelling is experimental. Neither mode translates continuous ASL grammar or every sign. Review the message before using it for important communication.
